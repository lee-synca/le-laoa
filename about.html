What Le Laoa is for

Le Laoa is a family page, designed as a central hub for all descendants of Su'e Tu'ulau to connect from around the world.

The videos here were recorded by our own family members so that the history, the gafa and the aganu'u are never lost — and so that every descendant, wherever they live, can learn them in our family's own voices. The two family e-books are free for every descendant to download and keep.

Please enjoy your time here, connect with your relatives and be respectful. If you have a minute, make sure you reach out to say “Hi”.
