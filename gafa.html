How to read a gafa

A gafa is written in a fixed formula. Learn these few words once, and the whole book opens up.

usu [name] ia [name]
The man named first married the woman named second.
usuia [name] e [name]
The woman is named first — she was married by the man named second.
f/g · fānau
The children born of that marriage (the book's own note: “f.g… Fanau/Fagau… Born”).
afafine o / alo o
Daughter of / child of — telling you whose family the wife comes from, and her village.
tama / teine
Boy / girl. “Tama uma” — all boys; “teine uma” — all girls.
suli
Heir — the one through whom the title and the line continue.
e pa
The line ended there — no heir was left to continue it.
usuga (1), (2)
First marriage, second marriage.
suafa
A chiefly title — here, the title Li‘o of Si‘umu.
tuafafine
Sister (of a man).
F.S. · Faife‘au Samoa
Church minister (the book's own note).
